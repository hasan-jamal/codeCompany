What Is AI-Driven Smart City Operations Solution?

AI-Driven Smart City Operations combine artificial intelligence, GIS, IoT, and integrated city platforms to manage urban services with real-time visibility and data-driven control.

A centralized Integrated Command and Control Centre (ICCC) provides a single operational view by unifying GIS data, AI analytics, IoT sensors, mobility systems, infrastructure, surveillance, and city services. This enables coordinated actions, faster incident response, and informed decision-making across city domains.

Through this unified command environment, operators gain centralized visibility and control, supporting responsive, coordinated, and data-driven urban operations.
