What Is AI Facility Management?

AI Facility Management uses artificial intelligence, IoT sensors, and advanced data analytics to continuously monitor facility conditions and automate operational decisions in real time.

From a single unified command centre, organizations can manage and optimize:

Operations

Day-to-day facility workflows

Safety

Maintenance

Predictive and condition-based upkeep

Space

Occupancy-based space utilization

Energy

Monitoring and optimization of consumption

Assets

Digital tracking of physical infrastructure
