CODE’s AI-powered Digital Twin technology creates a living, dynamic digital replica of your physical assets whether buildings, campuses, or even entire city infrastructure.

Unlike traditional 3D models, a Digital Twin is connected to real-time data, enabling you to continuously monitor, analyze, and optimize operations.

It helps you understand what’s happening inside your facilities right now, anticipate issues before they occur, and plan smarter for the future.

Whether you're managing energy, safety, space utilization, or system performance, the Digital Twin brings all your insights into one place: live, visual, and easily accessible.
