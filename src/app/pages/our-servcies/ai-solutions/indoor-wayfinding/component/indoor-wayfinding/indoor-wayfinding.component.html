Precision Navigation Inside Complex Facilities

CODE’s Smart Indoor Wayfinding solution provides precise and seamless navigation within large facilities even in areas where GPS doesn’t work.

Using advanced 3D laser scanning and a user-friendly digital assistant, the system delivers personalized, real-time guidance through hospitals, universities, service centers, and other complex environments.

With implementation possible in less than six months, this solution offers a fast and scalable way to enhance user experience, reduce staff workload and improve operational flow.

How It Works

3D Laser Scanning & Digital Twin Creation

We scan your entire facility using LiDAR technology, generating a high-accuracy (5–30 mm) 3D model and digital map.

Location Tagging & Map Integration

Points of interest — such as departments, rooms, or service desks — are tagged and embedded into the navigation system.

System Integration

The wayfinding map connects with existing building management systems, forming a unified operational layer.

Meet NaviBuddy — Your Digital Guide

Personalized Navigation Experience

Visitors choose their destination from a directory and are guided step-by-step using NaviBuddy — a friendly, AR-enabled mascot.

Augmented Reality Overlay

Through a smartphone camera, NaviBuddy appears in the real-world space to lead users visually and audibly.

Voice & Visual Sync

Clear voice instructions and visual animations help reduce navigation anxiety and ensure smooth movement.

Designed for Accessibility and Safety

Multi-Sensory Guidance

Augmented reality, voice prompts, and visual cues work together to support all users — including those with visual impairments.

Real-Time Emergency Routing

Automatically adapts routes during emergencies to guide users safely and efficiently.

Insight-Driven Analytics Dashboard

Leverage usage data and traffic patterns to optimize facility operations and enhance user flow.
