Secure, Private, and Digitally Managed Micro-Environments

CODE’s Smart Cabins are compact, digitally managed environments designed for privacy, precision, and performance.

Whether you’re conducting exams, holding interviews, or delivering sensitive citizen services, these AI-powered units ensure secure access, environmental control, and full digital traceability.


Integrated with biometric authentication, real-time monitoring, and acoustic insulation, Smart Cabins create the ideal micro-environment for education, healthcare, government, or enterprise use wherever control and confidentiality matter most.

Private. Secured. Always Connected.

Smart Access via Biometrics or QR

Authenticate users via fingerprint, ID card, or QR code — with full logging of access time, duration, and user identity.

Real-Time Visibility & Traceability

Built-in camera, microphone, and motion sensors provide full monitoring and session accountability.

Intelligent Environmental Monitoring

Continuously track temperature, air quality, CO₂, and humidity — with instant alerts for any irregularities.

Built for Continuity

Equipped with onboard UPS and edge computing to ensure uninterrupted operation — even during power or network outages.

Seamless AI Integration

Connects directly to CODE’s Digital Twin or Command Center for centralized control, advanced analytics, and automated alert handling.
