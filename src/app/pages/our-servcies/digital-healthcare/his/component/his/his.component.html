CODE’s Hospital Information System (HIS) delivers a unified digital foundation for modern healthcare — enabling full patient lifecycle management, real-time operational visibility, and data-driven clinical decisions.

Built on a secure, scalable, cloud-native architecture, the platform streamlines every hospital function — from admission and triage to labs, pharmacy, HR, and discharge — creating seamless collaboration across all departments.

By empowering Saudi hospitals to deliver high-impact care, improve outcomes, and scale confidently, CODE’s HIS supports sustainable growth and digital transformation in line with Vision 2030.
